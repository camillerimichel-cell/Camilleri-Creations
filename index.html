How We Build

Every app we make is guided by the people who use it.

Family First

Built by a parent, for families. We understand the chaos of daily life with children and design apps that reduce friction, not add to it.

Neurodiversity Aware

ADHD-friendly design runs through everything we build. Clear visual cues, one-thing-at-a-time focus, and no-pressure interactions.

Privacy Respecting

Data stays on your device wherever possible. No selling user data, no invasive tracking. Simple, transparent privacy policies.

Accessible by Default

High contrast options, reduced motion, VoiceOver support, and simplified UI modes are built in from day one — not bolted on later.
