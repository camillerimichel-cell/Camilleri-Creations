Our first glass-art drop is here

14 February 2026

After months of quiet evenings at the kiln, the first small collection of fused glass pieces is ready to share. This is a modest drop — a handful of pieces, each one made and finished by hand — but it marks the moment the studio side of Camilleri Creations steps out into the open.

Every piece in this first run started as sheets of coloured glass, cut and layered by eye, then fused over several hours in the kiln. No two come out identical. The colours shift depending on how the light lands, and the edges carry the soft rounding that only heat can give.

What's in the drop

The collection leans into Mediterranean colour — deep sea blues, warm turquoise, and the odd flash of Maltese red. You'll find small dishes, hanging pieces, and a few one-off experiments that didn't quite fit any category but were too good to melt back down.

Pricing isn't listed on the site by design. If a piece catches your eye, use the enquiry button on the glass gallery and I'll get back to you with details on that specific piece, including whether it's still available.

What comes next

This is the first of what I hope becomes a regular rhythm — make a small batch, share it, learn from it, repeat. If you'd like to follow along as new pieces come out of the kiln, the News & Blog is the place to check back.

Thank you for taking a look. It means a lot to finally share this part of the work.
