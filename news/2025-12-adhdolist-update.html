ADHDOList winter update and what's next

5 December 2025

The latest ADHDOList update is out, and it's a quieter release than usual — the kind that smooths edges rather than adding big new features. That was deliberate. Feedback this autumn kept pointing at the same thing: mornings work best when the app gets out of the way.

What's new in this release

  • Faster startup so the first prompt appears the moment you open the app.
  • Gentler audio cues with a new low-key option for households that prefer calm over urgency.
  • Clearer visual timers that stay readable at a glance, even from across the room.
  • Small fixes to the family race feature so scores sync more reliably between devices.

What's next

Looking ahead, the focus is on routines beyond the morning. A lot of families have asked for an evening wind-down flow, and that's the next big piece of work. Alongside it, I'm exploring simpler ways to build and reorder steps so a routine can be adjusted in seconds, not minutes.

As always, ADHDOList keeps data on-device wherever it can and asks for nothing it doesn't need. You can read more about the app on the ADHDOList page.

Thanks to everyone who sent notes and bug reports this season. The small, specific messages are the ones that shape releases like this one.
