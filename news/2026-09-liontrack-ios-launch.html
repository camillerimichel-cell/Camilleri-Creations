LionTrack is live on iOS — matchday stats meet gamified fun

28 September 2026

LionTrack is now available on the App Store. It's a grassroots youth football team manager built by a coach, for coaches — designed to get you off the spreadsheet and back onto the touchline where you belong.

If you coach a junior side, you know the admin creeps in from every direction: who turned up to training, who played where, who's on a hot streak, and how to keep a squad of energetic kids motivated week after week. LionTrack pulls all of that into one app that fits in your pocket.

Track the match as it happens

Record live match stats from the sideline, manage your squad, and log training attendance without breaking your focus on the game. Everything you capture builds a clear picture of each player's season, so when it's time to pick a player of the match or plan an awards night, the numbers are already there.

Get the kids excited to improve

LionTrack turns performance into progress the players actually care about. Award XP and badges, build streaks, and give every child a dashboard that celebrates what they're doing well. It's the difference between a stat sheet the coach keeps and a story the whole team wants to be part of.

Keep parents in the loop

Parents link to their child's profile and get a read-only dashboard showing XP, badges, match highlights, attendance, and streaks — so they can share in the journey without needing to chase you for updates.

Built to be inclusive

Accessibility was part of the plan from the start. LionTrack includes a neurodivergent mode with high contrast, reduced motion, and simplified UI options, plus five colour themes so the app works for the widest range of coaches, players, and families.

What you get

  • Live match tracking and player stats
  • XP, badges, and rewards to keep players motivated
  • Squad management and training attendance
  • Parent and player dashboards
  • Safeguarding tools and permission roles
  • Season PDF & CSV export
  • Home screen widgets and push notifications
  • Neurodivergent mode and five colour themes

Get LionTrack

Now live on iOS. Download it, track your next match, and let the badges do the motivating.
