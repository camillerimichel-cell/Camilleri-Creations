Our Books

Historical fiction in progress — rooted in real events, written for readers who love a puzzle, and each paired with a companion game.

The Crimson Hornet

One enemy network. Four books. A war fought in whispers.

Spy thriller Novel & graphic novel Writing in progress
Setting: 1941–1944 Places: Bletchley Park, London, Malta, Cairo, Dieppe

May 1941. As the battleship Bismarck slips out into the Atlantic, a young codebreaker at Bletchley Park notices a fragment of enemy signal traffic that should not exist — and, against orders, refuses to let it go. With a French intelligence officer attached to the Park, she comes to suspect that someone inside the Allied signals world is feeding convoy secrets to the enemy.

The Crimson Hornet follows the hunt for that network from the huts of Bletchley to besieged Malta, the officers' clubs of Cairo and the raid on Dieppe — a cat-and-mouse thriller against a hidden architect who always seems to be one move ahead, and a clock that never stops running.

The Series

  1. The Silent Frequency — outlined
  2. The Malta Gambit
  3. The False Objective
  4. The Last Cypher

What to Expect

  • 📡 Codebreaking & signals intelligence
  • ⚓ Atlantic convoys & the Bismarck chase
  • 🌍 Siege of Malta
  • 🕵️ Moles, double agents & tradecraft
  • ⏳ Race-against-the-clock pacing
  • 🎨 Graphic novel edition

Companion game: The Last Cypher — intercept, decrypt and deduce in the Lascaris War Rooms, Valletta, 1942.

Turgut's Legacy

A killer, a trail of relics, and a siege that never really ended.

Historical mystery Novel & graphic novel Writing in progress
Setting: 1908–1909, with interludes from 1565 Places: England, Germany, Italy, France, Spain, Venice, Malta

Late 1908. When a childhood friend who keeps the archives at St Albans Cathedral writes to her for help, a Wolverhampton analytical chemist arrives too late. She ropes in a linguist from the French embassy in London, and together they find a trail that leads far beyond England.

Someone is killing the descendants of the knights who defended Malta in the Great Siege of 1565 — and collecting the relics of the corsair Dragut, Turgut Reis, as they go. From Frankfurt and Turin through Avignon, Limoges, Toulouse, Zaragoza, Toledo and Carnival-season Venice to Malta, the pair are joined by a bibliophile aristocrat, a painter with a gift for ciphers and an adventurer raised on the Argentine plains, with a fast car and no fear of rooftops.

Interludes return to 1565, as Dragut sails for Malta and the Knights of St John brace for the siege that will decide the Mediterranean.

The Series (working title)

  1. St Albans to Toulouse — outlined · title to be announced
  2. Zaragoza to Malta — title to be announced

What to Expect

  • 🕵️ Five sleuths, five specialisms
  • 🏰 The Knights of St John
  • 🏴‍☠️ The corsair Dragut & the Great Siege
  • 🚂 Edwardian Europe by rail and motor car
  • 📜 Period books as chapter epigraphs
  • 🎨 Graphic novel edition

Companion game: Echoes of a Knight's Crusade — solve the ciphers and riddles yourself along the same route.
