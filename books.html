The Wayfinder Chronicles

Some people find their way. A few find the way between worlds.

Contemporary fantasy Coming of age Concept & story bible
Setting: Paris, 1994 — and the world beneath it Places: Paris and a hidden underground civilisation

Paris, 1994. A shy fifteen-year-old who would rather be lost in video games, history books and fantasy novels than at a party knows his city's streets, Metro lines and short cuts better than anyone. Then a discovery opens a door beneath the familiar city — not just catacombs and tunnels, but a vast inhabited world of kingdoms, guilds, forests, lakes, fortresses, ancient orders and strange creatures.

He learns that he is a Wayfinder, one of the rare few who can sense hidden routes and cross boundaries no one else can. Drawn into an ancient conflict with a manipulative enemy whose power was once held in check by a fragile alliance, he has to earn trust, build friendships and discover that finding a way is not the same as knowing what to do when you get there.

The Apprentice

From the forge to the front line — the making of a warrior.

Medieval historical adventure Concept & story bible
Setting: 1299–1301 Places: York, Northallerton and the North York Moors

York, 1299. Matthew, a fifteen-year-old blacksmith's apprentice, expects nothing grander than a forge of his own one day — until his master, short of money, sells him into the service of a rough, hard-drinking fighter to tend his horse and carry his gear. The drunk is not what he seems. He is a commander of a military order and a survivor of the Holy Land's last great sieges, a man who has lost his way but not his sword arm.

Trained by him and by a fierce young archer from the Yorkshire Dales, Matthew learns that mastery comes from practice, judgement and hard lessons, not destiny. When a remote settlement on the North York Moors begs for protection from a doomsday sect whose men seem to rise from the dead, the three of them have to stand between the village and the dark.

Echoes of a Knight's Crusade

A killer, a trail of relics, and a siege that never really ended.

Historical mystery Novel & graphic novel Writing in progress
Setting: 1908–1909, with scenes from the Great Siege of 1565 Places: England, Germany, Italy, France, Spain, Venice, Malta

Late 1908. A childhood friend who keeps the archives at St Albans Cathedral writes to a Wolverhampton analytical chemist for help — and she arrives too late. With a linguist from the French embassy in London, she follows a trail of murders and thefts that leads far beyond England.

Someone is gathering the belongings of the corsair Dragut, Turgut Reis, and the descendants of the knights who defended Malta in 1565 are dying for them. From Frankfurt, Turin, Bourges, Limoges and Toulouse to Zaragoza, Toledo, Carnival-season Venice and Malta, the pair are joined by a painter with a gift for ciphers, a bibliophile who would rather be in a library and a horsewoman who never refuses a rooftop — and each death forces them to ask what is owed to the dead.

Scenes from 1565 run through both books, as Dragut sails for Malta and the Knights of St John brace for the siege that will decide the Mediterranean.

The Crimson Hornet

One enemy network. Four books. A war fought in whispers.

Spy thriller Novel & graphic novel Writing in progress
Setting: 1941–1944 Places: Bletchley Park, London, Malta, Cairo

May 1941. As the battleship Bismarck slips out into the Atlantic, a young codebreaker at Bletchley Park notices a fragment of enemy signal traffic that should not exist — and, against orders, refuses to let it go. With a French intelligence officer attached to the Park, she comes to suspect that someone inside the Allied signals world is feeding convoy secrets to the enemy.

The Crimson Hornet follows the hunt for that network from the huts of Bletchley to besieged Malta, the officers' clubs of Cairo and back again for the days before D-Day — a cat-and-mouse thriller against a hidden architect who always seems to be one move ahead, and a clock that never stops running.

The Lives the Guns Stole

Four years of war. A bundle of letters. One promise to come home.

Historical fiction Wartime love story Novel & graphic novel Writing in progress
Setting: 1912–1918 Places: Codsall, Wolverhampton, Flanders, France, Malta

Summer 1914. A twenty-year-old farm hand from Codsall meets a trainee nurse in Wolverhampton, and the war interrupts them almost at once. He goes to the trenches with the South Staffords; she rises through the nursing ranks at the other end of the casualty lists, where his battles end up.

Across four years from Ypres to Cambrai, their letters are the one place each can still be the person they were in 1914, while the war takes friends, certainties and innocence. Told through his battles, her wards, their letters and the newspapers, orders and telegrams of the time, it is a story of growing up too fast — and of the lives the war kept from them.
