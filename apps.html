Artifact

Your portfolio command center — local-first, spec-driven.

macOS Windows

Artifact is a local-first cross-platform desktop app that serves as a portfolio command center for multi-platform app developers. It registers, tracks, searches, and visualises projects, specs, media assets, AI generation lineage, and store analytics — without compiling or running any child application code.

Register workspace directories and Artifact automatically discovers Kiro specs, parses requirements, tasks, and design documents, then populates a four-column pipeline board showing feature lifecycle status. An interactive dependency graph visualises task hierarchies and requirement cross-references.

A built-in credit ledger tracks AI generation costs across platforms (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Midjourney, Suno, Adobe Firefly) with manual entry and CSV import. Full-text search powered by FTS5 provides instant results across all indexed content.

Key Features

  • 📅 Pipeline Board (4-column Kanban)
  • 📈 Dependency Graph (React Flow)
  • 🔍 FTS5 Full-Text Search
  • 💰 Credit & Cost Ledger
  • 📂 Kiro Spec Parser
  • 🔌 Git Branch Detection
  • 📱 Context Slider (detail panel)
  • 💾 SQLite (local-first)
  • ⚡ Cmd+K Command Palette

Built with: Electron • React 19 • TypeScript • Tailwind CSS 4 • Vite • better-sqlite3 • React Flow • Zustand • recharts

LionTrack

Grassroots youth football team management — built by a coach, for coaches.

iOS Android

LionTrack is built for grassroots youth football coaches who want to focus on what matters: developing young players, not wrestling with spreadsheets.

Track attendance, record live match stats, manage squads, award XP and badges, and share progress with parents — all from one app that fits in your pocket on the touchline.

Designed with accessibility in mind, including a neurodivergent mode with high contrast, reduced motion, and simplified UI options. Parents link to their child's profile and get a read-only dashboard showing XP, badges, match highlights, attendance, and streaks.

ADHDOList

Making mornings manageable — one task at a time.

iOS Android

A guided morning routine app that helps children (and adults) with ADHD get through their mornings one task at a time. Visual countdown timers, audio prompts, and gamification keep things on track without the overwhelm.

Supports multiple family profiles, so everyone gets their own routines, streaks, and rewards. When multiple profiles run routines at the same time, a live Family Race appears showing everyone's progress in real time.

Designed with neurodiversity in mind: clear visual cues, one-thing-at-a-time focus, gamification that motivates without pressuring, and family features that make mornings feel less like a battle and more like a team effort.

Odyssey

Your fitness. Your epic journey.

iOS

Odyssey maps your cumulative exercise distance onto epic historical journey routes. The app integrates with Apple HealthKit for distance and vitals data, uses Firebase for backend persistence, and implements RPG-style progression including an avatar system, XP levelling, and streak tracking.

The MVP delivers the Ancient Rome route (Land's End to Rome, approximately 1,400 miles) with a Roman Legionary avatar. Your health metrics are displayed as RPG character stats in the Vitals Saga dashboard.

Key Features

  • 💖 Vitals Saga RPG stats
  • ⭐ XP & levelling
  • 🔥 Streak tracking

FirstSolo

Every pilot starts somewhere. Yours starts here.

iOS

FirstSolo is a gamified aviation learning app built specifically for RAF Air Cadets. It transforms the dense technical content of the Air Pilot's Manual (all seven volumes) into an engaging, structured journey from a cadet's first parade night to their first solo flight.

Seven narrative chapters map to the seven APM volumes, culminating in a FirstSolo finale — a full simulated solo flight that tests knowledge from all volumes in a single integrated scenario.

Key Features

  • 📚 7 narrative chapters (APM Vols 1-7)
  • 🏆 Classification badges
  • ✈️ Simulated solo flight finale
  • 🎓 CAA PPL exam alignment
  • 🔮 Gamified learning
  • 👥 Squadron leaderboards
  • ♿ Neurodivergent-friendly design
  • 🔒 Squadron/wing subscriptions

Built with: Swift/SwiftUI • Firebase (Firestore, Auth, Functions, Analytics)

Provenance

Invest in what you know. Discover what you don't.

iOS

Provenance is an investment discovery and education app that starts with what users already know — the products they buy, the brands they love, the hobbies they pursue — and traces the financial thread from personal passion to investable opportunity.

The app is driven by five personal signals: Passion Profile, Spend Intelligence (via Open Banking), Conviction Score, Ethics Rating, and a Living Portfolio that evolves with the user's life.
