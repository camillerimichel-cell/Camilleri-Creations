Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." This will check your Apple ID or Google Play account for existing subscriptions and re-activate them.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account from within the app (Settings > Account > Delete Account). Alternatively, email us at camillericreations@icloud.com and we'll process it within 30 days.

Which devices are supported?

Our iOS apps require iOS 17 or later and work on iPhone. Some apps (FirstSolo, FamilyHub) have optimised iPad layouts. Android apps require Android 10 or later.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play Store — not through our apps directly. Go to your device Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions (iOS) or Google Play > Payments & subscriptions (Android).

Is my data backed up?

Apps that use Firebase (LionTrack, FamilyHub, StudyTrack, Odyssey, FirstSolo, Provenance, Fancy That) sync data to the cloud automatically. Your data is available on any device you sign into. ADHD Morning Routine stores data locally with optional iCloud/Firebase sync for family features.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. All our apps work offline for core functionality. Data will sync when you reconnect to the internet. Some features (family race, team sync, AI-generated content) require an active connection.

I have a billing issue. Who do I contact?

All billing is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). For refunds or payment issues, contact Apple Support or Google Play Support directly. We don't have access to payment information.

Tips for Reporting Bugs

The more detail you include, the faster we can fix the issue. Here's what helps:

Include in your email:

1. Which app and version (found in Settings > About)
2. Your device model and OS version (e.g., iPhone 15, iOS 17.5)
3. What you were doing when the problem occurred
4. What you expected to happen vs what actually happened
5. Screenshots or screen recordings if possible
